MikkiLens

Privacy policy

MikkiLens runs on your own computer. There is no MikkiLens account, no MikkiLens server, and nothing about you is sent to us — because there is no “us” for it to be sent to.

Last updated 6 September 2026

The short version

  • MikkiLens is a Windows application you install and run yourself. It has no backend of its own.
  • We collect nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no account to sign up for.
  • Everything it saves — your settings, your YouTube sign-in, its log — is saved in a folder on your computer.
  • It talks to other services because you asked it to: YouTube, the voice that reads to you, and anything you configure yourself. Those are listed below, with what each one receives.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or used to train anything.

Google user data

When you press Connect and sign in, Google asks whether you want to let MikkiLens manage your YouTube account. This section says exactly what that means in practice.

What is requested, and why

MikkiLens requests one scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube, which Google describes as “manage your YouTube account”. It is the narrowest scope that covers everything the application does. In particular it does not request youtube.force-ssl, which would include the right to permanently delete your videos, ratings, comments and captions — MikkiLens never needs that and never asks for it.

These are all of the calls it makes, and what each one is for:

CallWhat it is used for
liveBroadcasts.list Answering “am I live?”, and finding the broadcast whose chat should be read.
liveBroadcasts.update Changing your stream title when you ask it to. This is the only call that writes anything, and the title is the only thing it writes.
liveChatMessages.list Reading your live chat aloud. Used as a fallback: chat normally comes from the public live chat page instead, to stay within the YouTube API quota.
videos.list The number of people watching.
channels.list Which channel you are signed in as, so it can say the name back to you and keep more than one channel apart.
search.list Finding your current live broadcast when it is not otherwise listed.

What is stored, and where

Your Google sign-in is stored as a token file in MikkiLens’s own data folder on your computer (data\youtube_token.json, or one file per channel under data\youtube\ if you connect more than one). Alongside it, MikkiLens keeps the channel id and channel name so it can tell you which account it is on.

That token is sent to Google, and to nobody else, ever. It is not uploaded, backed up, or synchronised anywhere. Anyone with access to your computer and that folder can use it, in the same way they could use your browser if you were signed in there.

What is done with the data that comes back

Chat messages, viewer counts, broadcast titles and channel names are read aloud to you and kept in memory while the application is running. Chat messages are not written to disk. The application’s log (data\mikkilens.log) records what MikkiLens did and what it said, which can include the text of a message it read out; it stays on your computer and you can delete it at any time.

Text that MikkiLens speaks aloud is turned into speech by Microsoft’s Edge read-aloud service, which means the words being spoken are sent there. That includes chat messages and donation messages while they are being read. See the list of services below.

Taking it back

You can withdraw MikkiLens’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Inside the application, the settings page has a Disconnect button that deletes the stored token from your computer. Deleting the data folder, or uninstalling MikkiLens, removes it as well.

Limited Use

MikkiLens’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain terms: data from Google is used only to provide the features you asked for, on your own machine. It is not sold, not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train any model. No human at MikkiLens reads it, because MikkiLens never receives it.

YouTube API Services

MikkiLens uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s own handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Everything MikkiLens talks to

MikkiLens is your own client for other people’s services. Each of these is contacted directly from your computer, and only for the feature next to it.

ServiceWhat it receives, and when
YouTube / Google Your signed-in requests, as described above. Live chat is normally read from the public live chat page of your own broadcast.
Microsoft (Edge read-aloud) The text to be spoken, every time MikkiLens says something — confirmations, chat messages, donation messages. No account is used and nothing identifies you beyond the request itself. Spoken phrases are cached in data\tts_cache so repeated ones are not sent again.
YouTube Music The song title you searched for, when you search for music.
DuckDuckGo The search text, when you ask MikkiLens to look something up on the web.
An AI endpoint you configure Only if you fill one in. It receives a screenshot when you ask what is on the screen, chat text when you ask for a summary, and the sentence you said when no command matched it. Empty by default, in which case those features are off.
Tako, Trakteer Only if you paste your overlay link. MikkiLens watches that link for donation alerts, exactly as the browser source in OBS does.
GitHub An update check against MikkiLens’s public releases, and the download of the update if you accept it. Also where the two music programs (yt-dlp and ffmpeg) come from.
Hugging Face The download of the speech-recognition model, once, at first launch.

Your microphone

Speech recognition runs on your own computer by default: the audio of what you said is turned into text locally and is not sent anywhere. It is not recorded to disk. If you deliberately configure a remote speech-recognition endpoint in the settings, the audio goes to that endpoint instead — that is the only case in which your voice leaves the machine.

Your screen

A screenshot is taken only when you ask what is on the screen, and only then. It is sent to the AI endpoint you configured, and not stored. With no endpoint configured — the state MikkiLens ships in — nothing is captured at all.

What is kept on your computer

Everything MikkiLens writes lives in its own folder:

config.tomlYour settings.
data\youtube_token.json
data\youtube\		Your YouTube sign-in, one file per channel.
data\secrets.tomlAPI keys you entered, kept out of the settings file so that file stays safe to share when you need help.
data\mikkilens.logWhat the application did, for working out what went wrong.
data\tts_cacheSpoken phrases, so common ones are instant.
data\modelsThe speech-recognition and wake-word files.

All of it is removed by deleting that folder or uninstalling the application. Nothing is kept anywhere else, because there is nowhere else.
