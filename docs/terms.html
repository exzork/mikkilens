1. What MikkiLens is

MikkiLens is a Windows application that lets you operate OBS and your own YouTube live stream by voice, and that reads what is happening back to you out loud. It runs entirely on your computer. There is no hosted service behind it, no account to create, and nothing of yours is stored anywhere but on your own machine — see the privacy policy for the detail.

3. Quotas and limits

The YouTube Data API has a daily quota. MikkiLens is written to stay well inside it — live chat is normally read from the public chat page rather than the API, and the application tracks its own usage — but the quota belongs to the Google Cloud project the sign-in was issued against, and running out of it means some features stop answering until it resets. That is a limit of the service, not a fault in the application.
