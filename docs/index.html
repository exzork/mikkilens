MikkiLens

Run the stream by voice, and hear it answer.

A Windows application for streamers who cannot watch the screen while they stream. Say what you want; MikkiLens does it and says back what happened.

MikkiLens was written for one streamer who is blind, and for the people who help her set up. Everything it does has to work by ear: there is a tone for every state, a sentence for every result, and nothing that has to be found on a screen. It controls OBS, reads her YouTube live chat aloud, announces donations, describes what is on the screen when she asks, and finds and plays music — all from spoken commands and a few keys.

It runs on her own computer. There is no MikkiLens account, no MikkiLens server, and nothing is sent anywhere except to the services she connects it to herself.

What it does

Runs the stream Starts and stops the broadcast, switches OBS scenes, mutes the microphone, shows and hides sources, changes the stream title, reads out the viewer count.
Reads the chat Reads YouTube live chat aloud, at its own speed and volume, with a key to silence it for a moment without losing anything.
Announces donations Waits for the alert on screen to finish, then reads out who gave and what they said.
Describes the screen Sends a screenshot to the AI endpoint you configure, and says back what is on it. Nothing is configured by default.
Plays music Finds a song on YouTube Music and plays it inside MikkiLens, so it can be paused, restarted, and turned down under her voice.

Connecting YouTube

MikkiLens asks to sign in to YouTube so that it can do the things above on your own channel: read whether you are live, read your live chat, tell you how many people are watching, and change your stream title when you ask it to. It asks for the narrowest permission that covers those, and it never asks for the right to delete anything.

Your Google sign-in is stored on your own computer and used only to talk to YouTube. You can take it back at any time from your Google account's permissions page. The privacy policy says exactly what is stored, where, and who else the application talks to.
